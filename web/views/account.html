Account

Session cookies stay on this gateway only (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict).

Reset credentials

  1. On the TTGO, hold the bottom button (GPIO0) for about 3 seconds until the reset prompt appears.
  2. Press the top button (GPIO35) to confirm wiping the stored login, or bottom to cancel.
  3. Open the dashboard again and create a new username/password, then confirm on the device.

Password rules

  • Username: up to 32 characters — letters, digits, . _ - only.
  • Password: 8–64 printable ASCII characters (no control / non-ASCII bytes).

Network

The UI is served over HTTP on the LAN (http://<ip>:6789). TLS was tried on this board but freezes the gateway when combined with BLE — keep the port off the public internet.
