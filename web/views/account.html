Account

Session cookies stay on this gateway only (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict).

Guest access

Off, and no tab is ticked, until you change it. The choice is stored on the SD card. The camera stays signed in. A guest does not receive addresses, the IP, or ports.

Reset credentials

  1. On the P4, open Account and tap Reset login, then tap the confirm bar (Cancel leaves the login in place).
  2. On the M5, hold A about 3 seconds, then confirm with B.
  3. Create a new username and password in this browser, then confirm on the device.

Password rules

  • Username: up to 32 characters — letters, digits, . _ - only.
  • Password: 8–64 printable ASCII characters (no control / non-ASCII bytes).
